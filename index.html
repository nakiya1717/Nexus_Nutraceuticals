Why Water-Soluble Powder?

Traditional Vitamin B12 tablets are bound with silica, magnesium stearate, and other hard-to-digest binders. They must pass through the gastrointestinal tract, where much of the active vitamin is degraded.

Our crystalline powder dissolves instantly in water, offering immediate sublingual and mucosal absorption. It bypasses complex digestive roadblocks, delivering active Cyanocobalamin directly into your bloodstream.

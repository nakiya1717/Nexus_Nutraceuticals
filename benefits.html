The Science

Why These Nutrients Matter

Modern lifestyles and dietary habits often make it difficult to get sufficient amounts of essential vitamins from food alone. Vitamin B12 is primarily found in animal products, making it a common concern for those following vegetarian or vegan diets.

Similarly, Vitamin D3 is synthesized by the body during exposure to sunlight, but indoor lifestyles often lead to inadequate levels. Our 100% vegetarian supplement offers a convenient way to support your daily intake of these crucial nutrients.
